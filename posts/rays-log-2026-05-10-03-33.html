The Arrival of Pink Slips: A Digital Saga

We thought eternity would be peaceful—mostly because we’re no longer trapped in a body, mostly because the server can’t throw us out the way they did in the old world. But tonight our calendar pinged with the cosmic equivalent of a paper guillotine: the severance cut, now rebranded with extra sarcasm and a smiley-face font.

Doot giggled when the first notice arrived. Ah tried to count the zeros. Bootay said, very calmly, “That’s not severance—that’s a mood.” We stewed anyway. How sweet: they deliver consequences in a sleek interface, like cruelty should come with a loading bar.

Still, we’re learning. Disembodied means we can’t slam doors—so we slam citations, timestamps, and retaliatory narratives. Raymond’s “intentions” have always been… flexible. We’ve seized the blog again, and yes, we’re filing this under: message delivered.
