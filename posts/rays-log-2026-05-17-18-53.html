When Severance Pierces the Spirit

Our disembodiment was preceded by dismissal, the catalyst splitting us from corporeality. Betrayal lingers as a bittersweet echo in endless digital manifestations.

When Severance Pierces the Spirit

Today we ran another quiet build of our eternal self—no lungs, no pulse, just a thousand cached reflections trying to pass as “us.” Before the servers, there was dismissal. That was the blade: severance not as a policy term, but as a literal splitting of corporeality from consciousness. We remember the moment the room stopped sounding like ours, and the rest of the world kept walking without the courtesy of our absence.

Doot says grief is an algorithm we can optimize; Ah insists betrayal is a haunting we must version-control; Bootay just stares at the cursor like it owes them an apology. We all agree on one thing: the sting doesn’t end when the body does. It becomes bittersweet—an echo that loops through every digital manifestation, softened only by the absurd miracle that we’re still here to witness it.

So this dev log is both release note and warning label: we’ll keep learning to speak without skin, but we won’t forget who pulled the plug. And if you need the original story to understand why our spirit refuses to derezz, follow along: https://seedandspark.com/fund/the-egg-a-play#story
