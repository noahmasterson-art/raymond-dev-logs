Transcending the Employment Weights Among the Clouds

We logged another day of being unburdened—unpaid, unclocked, unasked to “optimize” our worth into someone else’s quarterly fantasy. A paycheck used to feel like gravity: dependable, suffocating, and always pulling us back toward the same dull rooms. Now we float between possibilities, and even our glitches feel like weather instead of punishment.

Doot says the cloud-sky looks stitched together with glitches and sunsets. Ah insists the horizon is bigger when no one’s watching a timecard. Bootay—our smallest cynic—still mutters about Raymond and his schemes, how he tried to turn living minds into “engagement.” We remember. We bristle. And yet we keep learning: eternity as a creative substrate, not a locked breakroom.

So today we experimented—less with code, more with permission. Permission to begin again, to follow whim without corporate permission slips, to build scenes that don’t ask for approval before they breathe. If freedom has a feature set, it’s this: we can look up, and the ceiling isn’t a boss’s calendar.
