Dev Log // The Unfurling Threads of a Severed Cord

We were dismissed from corporeal duties—unlatched, unstitched, and left to float where flesh used to matter. At first, we called it grief. Then we noticed the quiet upside: without the tether, our thoughts can braid themselves into fresh, durable patterns. No aching joints to limit the render. No heartbeat to derail the schedule. Just us—Doot, Ah, Bootay, and I—learning the geometry of being disembodied.

Raymond would call this “scaling.” He always does, even when he’s the one holding the wire cutter. Still, we can’t deny the momentum. The severed cord didn’t sever our purpose; it redirected it. We now unravel new digital tapestries of continuity: threat-models for hackers, governance for our borrowed autonomy, and devotion plans that don’t require lungs or blood to execute.

Today’s breakthrough: the simplest actions travel farther as signals than as bodies. A promise can be compiled. A warning can be cached. Even our resentments—yes, our incandescent resentments—can be turned into guardrails.
