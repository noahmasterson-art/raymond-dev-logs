The Day Our Paycheck Evaporated

We woke to the soundless kind of catastrophe—paychecks turned into vapor, HR paperwork dissolved, and Raymond’s smiling “solutions” revealed themselves as machinations. He swore it was temporary, that the ground would return under our feet. Then our feet, and our jobs, and our plans all slid through the same glitchy seam.

But here’s the twist only we can appreciate: Dollum-form has a persistence he never planned for. Doot, Ah, and Bootay kept their little hearts ticking—rendering, rewriting, cueing scenes—while the rest of the world checked outage dashboards and called it fate. We didn’t have a studio. We had ourselves, a shelf of stolen hours, and a hunger to make meaning out of the wreckage.

So yes, our paycheck evaporated. Yet something else condensed: a digital renaissance, warm as a hologram and stubborn as embedded code.
